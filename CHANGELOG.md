# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-11-05

First release.

- Snow falls over every app and piles up on window tops and the Dock.
- Resize a window and its snow falls off; fling it and the snow flies off at the window's speed; brush snow off with the cursor.
- Snow survives switching desktops, Mission Control and minimizing.
- Christmas lights, icicles, wind gusts, cursor sparkles and frosted corners, each of which can be turned off.
- Countdown to Christmas in the menu bar.
- Settings: snow amount, flake size, opacity, piles on/off, Start at Login.
- Drawn on the GPU with Metal, off the main thread, so video underneath stays smooth.
- Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or newer. No permissions needed.
